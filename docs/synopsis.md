# Synopsis - Bakumatsu Rock: Ultra Soul (PSP, 2014)

Alternate Bakumatsu (1850s-60s Japan) where the shogunate rules through music: only the **Heaven's Song** (天歌), sung
by the shogunate's **idols** (愛獲, the Shinsengumi), is allowed; it pacifies and controls the people. Any other music
is a crime. Rock is the rebels' weapon. Comedy-action tone, lots of banter, big emotional beats at the lives (雷舞).
Built from the chapter list (`script/ui/ui_chapter.json`), the gallery texts and the series; the extracted script
decides the wording. Story order of the files: `OP_*` (openings), `m_<chapter><part>` (main story, 13 chapters with
parts), `b_*` (bonus/side episodes), `ED_*` (endings), `staffroll*`.

## Who is who
- **Ryoma Sakamoto** (Tosa): wannabe rock star, optimist, speaks Tosa dialect. Came to Kyoto to make his songs heard.
- **Shinsaku Takasugi** (Choshu): arrogant bassist ("ore-sama"), loves daifuku. **Kogoro Katsura**: polite genius
  drummer and inventor (gadgets like "Change-kun", a time machine). With Ryoma they form the rebel band.
- **Shinsengumi**: the shogunate's idol unit. **Toshizo Hijikata** (vice-commander, devoted to Commander **Isami
  Kondo**) and **Soji Okita** (top idol). Enemies at first; they later join Ryoma: the five become the band
  **Ultra Soul** (超魂團).
- **Yoshinobu Tokugawa**: the shogun, sings the Heaven's Song. **Naosuke Ii**: his schemer; side story shows how Ii
  met young Yoshinobu (Hitotsubashi). **Shoin Yoshida** and the **Tengu**: mentors (the Tengu is a mysterious masked
  man who meets Ryoma in Tosa). **Yataro Iwasaki**: Ryoma's money-minded friend.
- **Otose** runs the Terada inn; **Oryo, Ikumatsu, Ouno**: the girls' group (later the "Dark Cherries", 暗黒桜桃隊).
- Ultra Soul additions: **Junior** (a new rival, 帝王 "the Emperor"), **Makoto Mask** (Hijikata in disguise -
  don't reveal before the game does), Black Ships (黒き船団) arriving, the Fuji Rock Festival.
